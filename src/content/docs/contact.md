## Design partners & early access

ctxmesh is built in the open and evolving quickly. If you're running agents in
production — or trying to — a short conversation goes a long way: we learn what
breaks at your scale, and you get an early say in where the platform goes.

Good things to include: what your agents do, how you run them today, and the
governance or reliability gap that brought you here.
